# StereoFine 1.4

- Straighten the horizon and correct vertical perspective with **H**. The current stereo preview and grid controls remain available while adjusting. Press Enter to realign the pair or Esc to discard the draft.
- Retain inspection zoom when realigning the current pair.
- Prepare neighbouring previews in the background and show ready previews directly.
- Analyse pairs in parallel in batch mode when resources allow.
- Adjust exposure per pair or apply the current exposure to a folder.
- Hold Shift to adjust exposure and LUT strength with the mouse wheel.
