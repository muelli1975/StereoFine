# Supplied colour looks

StereoFine's application code remains MIT licensed. The supplied tables retain their individual terms. Preset names identify colour looks, not endorsements by film or camera manufacturers.

## G'MIC Cube exports

David Tschumperlé published the [G'MIC external data archive](https://gmic.eu/gmic_all_data.zip) under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://github.com/GreycLab/gmic-community/issues/15#issuecomment-131894845). Its `gmic_cluts.gmz` index contains all 39 G'MIC presets supplied here. The [G'MIC gallery exporter](https://github.com/GreycLab/gmic/blob/36945644520d75d593a1802ea4db2c64ea126e54/src/gmic_stdlib.gmic#L11325-L11355) resamples these tables and publishes them as Cube files. The archive license covers these exports. The original sources and their individual license declarations are credited below. StereoFine supplies the G'MIC Cube files unchanged.

## Film simulations — CC BY-SA 4.0

The film, instant-film, monochrome and cross-process simulations from the RawTherapee Film Simulation Collection credit Pat David, Pavlov Dmitry and Michael Ezra. The Cube files are the unchanged conversions published by G'MIC. See the source and original film reference for each look in `manifest.json`. The collection is mirrored at https://github.com/NatronGitHub/clut and the distributor's original notice is `NATRON_README.md`. The full [CC BY-SA 4.0 terms](../../licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) are included.
